# Task 5: Real-World Deployment Ideas for a PDF Chatbot

## 1. IRCTC: Rail Safety & Circulars PDF Chatbot
- **PDF Document Source:** Massive 200+ page Indian Railways passenger rulebooks, circulars on Tatkal cancellation fees, refund rules for delayed trains, and luggage weight limit circulars.
- **How It Works:** When passengers upload or query ticket disputes, the PDF chatbot retrieves the exact circular clause and explains the refund entitlement or quota policy in plain language.
- **User Benefit:** Passengers no longer need to scour through confusing legal PDFs or argue at railway counters; they receive immediate, verifiable policy quotes with clause numbers.

---

## 2. Zomato: Restaurant Hygiene & Lab Audit Report Inspector
- **PDF Document Source:** FSSAI compliance certificates, food safety inspection audit reports, and allergen lab test PDF sheets published by cloud kitchens and restaurants.
- **How It Works:** A user with strict dietary allergies (e.g., peanut, gluten, lactose) or hygiene concerns can chat with the restaurant's published FSSAI compliance PDF.
- **User Benefit:** High-risk allergic customers can verify: *"Does this bakery's kitchen handle tree nuts on shared prep tables according to their last audit?"*, drastically improving food safety and consumer trust.

---

## 3. BookMyShow: Venue Policies, Layouts & Festival Guidelines Assistant
- **PDF Document Source:** Extensive event handbook PDFs for major stadium concerts, IPL matches, and multi-day music festivals (e.g., Lollapalooza India).
- **How It Works:** Attendees ask questions like: *"Which gate is accessible for wheelchair seating?"*, *"Are DSLR cameras or power banks permitted inside Narendra Modi Stadium?"*, or *"What is the parking entry pass guideline?"*
- **User Benefit:** Thousands of concert-goers avoid long queues and bag check confiscations by getting instant, accurate answers extracted directly from official event handbook PDFs.
